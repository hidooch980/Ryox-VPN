# Privacy Policy — RYOX VPN

**Effective Date:** 2026

RYOX VPN is built from the ground up as a 100% free, open-source, anti-surveillance privacy tool.

## Absolute Zero-Logs Commitment

- **No Traffic Logging**: We do not monitor, log, capture, or store the websites you visit, the data you transfer, or your connection destinations.
- **No Personal Information**: RYOX VPN does not require an account, email address, credit card, phone number, or any identity information to use.
- **No Analytics / No Telemetry**: There are no third-party analytics SDKs, Google Analytics, Firebase, or hidden telemetry beacons in the software.
- **No Ads**: RYOX VPN does not contain advertisements or ad network trackers.
- **Local Storage Only**: All your saved servers, subscriptions, and custom rules are stored locally on your device in plain JSON format.

## Third-Party Feeds & Free Sources

When using the Free Sources feature, your client fetches public subscription text files directly from public open-source repositories (such as GitHub raw URLs). RYOX VPN does not intermediary-proxy or log these requests.
